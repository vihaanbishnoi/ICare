How It Works

A five-stage pipeline from raw video frame to fall probability

01

Frame Capture

Frames processed at up to 12 FPS. Stale frames are dropped — latency never accumulates.

→
02

Person Detection

YOLOX-tiny detects person every 3rd frame; bounding-box reused between detections.

→
03

Pose Estimation

RTMPose-s estimates 17 COCO body keypoints per frame with per-joint confidence scores.

→
04

Temporal Buffer

Rolling 4-second buffer resampled to 48 positions → 17×48×64×64 joint heatmaps.

→
05

PoseC3D ONNX

Fine-tuned model outputs P(Fall), P(No Fall) at ~1.33 predictions/s. Threshold 0.50 triggers incident.

Incident rule: P(Fall) ≥ 0.50 triggers an incident. Re-arms after 3 predictions below 0.35. Alert latency is recorded only when an independent ground-truth fall-onset annotation is available.

Known Limitations

Honest research boundaries — read before drawing conclusions

👤

Single Person Only

Tracks only the largest detected person. Multi-person identity tracking is not implemented.

📊

Group-Aware, Not Subject-Independent

Prevents groups from crossing folds. Subject IDs unavailable — results may not generalise to unseen individuals.

⚡

CPU Throughput Gap

Pose inference runs slower than camera FPS on CPU. Measured FPS, RAM, and latency are pending live-service testing.

🔬

Train/Deploy Domain Gap

RTMPose at deployment may differ from the pose generator used during training. Gap not yet quantified.

🚫

Not a Medical Device

ICare is a research prototype. Must not be used as an emergency response or clinical monitoring system.

📐

Urgency Not Yet Adaptive

Motion urgency and reliability are instrumented but do not control model scheduling. Thresholds await validation traces.
